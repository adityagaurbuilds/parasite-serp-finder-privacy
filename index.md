Privacy Policy for Parasite SEO SERP Finder
Parasite SEO SERP Finder (“the extension”) is a browser extension that labels search results on Google, Bing, and DuckDuckGo that come from subdomain and user-content platforms. This policy explains what data it handles.
In short: the extension works entirely on your device. It does not send data to the developer or to anyone else, and it makes no network requests of its own.
What the extension reads: When you view a search results page on a supported site, the extension reads the search query in the page address, and the links and titles of the results shown. It uses these only to label results and to show you a summary. It does not read any other website, and it does not read your accounts, passwords, cookies, or form entries.
What the extension stores on your device
• Your settings: the sites you list as your own, any extra platforms you add, and your on/off options. These are saved with Chrome’s storage.sync API. If you have Chrome Sync turned on, Chrome may sync these settings between your own devices through your Google account. The developer has no access to them.
• A local history of searches you have viewed: the query, the date, and the position, type, address, and title of each flagged result. Up to the 300 most recent searches are kept.
• Searches you choose to save, including any notes you type.
This data is kept with Chrome’s local storage on your device. You can delete the history in the extension’s Settings (Clear saved history), remove saved searches individually, or remove everything by uninstalling the extension.
What is not collected: The extension does not collect personal information, does not use analytics or tracking, does not show ads, does not use cookies, and does not sell or share any data with third parties. It does not load or run remote code.
Permissions
• storage: to keep the settings, history, and saved searches described above.
• Access to Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo search results pages: to read result links and titles and add labels. The extension does not run on other sites.
Children: The extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle information from children.
